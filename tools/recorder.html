Before you start (1 minute)
  • Use Chrome. The first time you press Record, allow microphone access.
  • Quiet room, laptop on a table, about an arm's length from you. Keep the same position for all 40 clips.
  • Read each sentence naturally, at normal speed, the way you would talk to your phone. Don't over-pronounce the English words.
  • Leave about one second of silence before you speak and after you finish, then stop.
  • Listen back. If you stumbled, press Record again to replace the take.
  • Clips are kept in this browser if you close the tab, but download the ZIP as soon as you finish.
