## Weighted STT score per hardware profile

Weights from the research plan: accuracy 30% (Svarah WER), speed 25% (median latency, log scale), licence 20%, Indian/Tamil readiness 15% (own-clip WER and Tamil support, half each), deployability 10% (no hallucination without extra components = 1, needs a VAD = 0.5). Each criterion is min-max scaled across the candidates on that profile (best = 1). Sensitivity: the ranking with accuracy at 50% (speed 15%, licence 15%, readiness 15%, deployability 5%). Hard gates, applied before scoring: commercial-use licence (all STT candidates pass) and real time (RTF below 1).
