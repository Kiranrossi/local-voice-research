# Test sentences (gold set)

Sentences a user might say to a voice assistant: booking, payments, timings, complaints, everyday requests.

**Script convention:** Tamil words in Tamil script, English words in Latin script.

**Provenance:** first draft written with AI assistance (Claude), then reviewed and edited by a native Tamil speaker so every sentence reads like natural spoken Tamlish. This is noted in the report's AI-tools section. The audio is recorded by a human speaker; nothing in the test set is synthetic speech.
